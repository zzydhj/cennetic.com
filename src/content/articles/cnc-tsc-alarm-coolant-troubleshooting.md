  <h2 id="why-does-the-cnc-through-spindle-coolant-alarm-trigger">Why Does the CNC Through Spindle Coolant Alarm Trigger?</h2>
  <p>According to cutting tool experts at Sandvik Coromant, high-pressure coolant systems (typically running at 15 to 70 bar) significantly increase tool life and chip evacuation. However, this high pressure requires a perfectly sealed and filtered system. A CNC TSC alarm usually indicates a <strong>TSC pressure error</strong>—meaning the sensor has detected that the coolant pressure has fallen below the safe operating threshold (often around 10 bar).</p>
  <p>The three most common culprits are:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Clogged Filtration:</strong> Fine metal chips or degraded coolant sludge blocking the inline filters.</li>
    <li><strong>Rotary Union Failure:</strong> Worn seals in the spindle’s rotary union allowing high-pressure coolant to leak.</li>
    <li><strong>Pump Cavitation or Sensor Drift:</strong> Air trapped in the coolant lines or a faulty pressure transducer sending false signals to the CNC controller.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 id="frequently-asked-questions-faq">Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)</h2>
  
  <h3 id="1-how-do-i-clear-a-cnc-tsc-alarm-on-my-machine">1. How do I clear a CNC TSC alarm on my machine?</h3>
  <p>First, stop the machine and check the coolant tank level. Next, inspect the inline filter for blockages and clean it if necessary. Verify that you are using a tool holder with a through-hole pull-stud. Once the issue is resolved, press the reset button on your CNC controller.</p>

  <h3 id="2-can-the-wrong-type-of-coolant-cause-a-tsc-pressure-error">2. Can the wrong type of coolant cause a TSC pressure error?</h3>
  <p>Absolutely. Coolants that foam excessively or lack proper lubricity can cause the high-pressure pump to cavitate, leading to pressure drops. Always use high-quality, low-foaming coolant recommended for high-pressure systems.</p>

  <h3 id="3-how-often-should-the-rotary-union-be-replaced">3. How often should the rotary union be replaced?</h3>
  <p>The lifespan of a rotary union depends on the operating pressure and coolant cleanliness. With proper filtration, a high-quality rotary union on a QiaoFeng machine can last several years. If you notice coolant leaking from the top of the spindle, it is time for a replacement.</p>
