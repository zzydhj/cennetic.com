  <h2 id="6-vmc-buying-checklist-6-steps-before-you-order">6. VMC Buying Checklist: 6 Steps Before You Order</h2>
  <ol>
    <li><strong>Define your workpiece envelope:</strong> Measure your largest part and add 20% clearance to determine minimum X/Y/Z travel requirements.</li>
    <li><strong>Identify your primary material:</strong> Aluminum/non-ferrous → prioritize spindle RPM (V-856). Steel/cast iron → prioritize rigidity and table load (L-1060).</li>
    <li><strong>Check your floor load capacity:</strong> The L-1060 weighs 8T — confirm your workshop floor can support it before ordering.</li>
    <li><strong>Choose your CNC controller:</strong> FANUC is the global standard for serviceability. Siemens is preferred in Europe. Mitsubishi is popular across Asia.</li>
    <li><strong>Request a pre-delivery QC report:</strong> Always ask for the laser interferometer and ballbar test report. QIAOFENG provides full QC documentation with every machine.</li>
    <li><strong>Confirm warranty and support terms:</strong> All QIAOFENG vertical machining centers carry a <strong>2-year warranty</strong> with lifetime technical support and 24/7 remote assistance.</li>

  <h2 id="7-frequently-asked-questions">7. Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

  <h3 id="what-is-the-difference-between-a-linear-guideway-and-a-box-g">What is the difference between a linear guideway and a box guideway on a vertical machining center?</h3>
  <p><strong>Linear guideways</strong> (V-856, JR-V85P) offer lower friction, higher rapid traverse speeds, and better positioning accuracy — ideal for high-speed precision work. <strong>Box guideways</strong> (L-1060 Z-axis) provide greater contact area and vibration damping, making them superior for heavy-duty cutting with high radial loads on steel and cast iron.</p>

  <h3 id="which-cnc-controller-options-are-available-for-qiaofeng-vmcs">Which CNC controller options are available for QIAOFENG VMCs?</h3>
  <p>The V-856 and L-1060 are available with <strong>FANUC 0i-MF Plus</strong>, <strong>Siemens 828D</strong>, or <strong>Mitsubishi M80</strong> as standard options. The JR-V85P is a bare machine — you supply and integrate your own preferred controller.</p>

  <h3 id="what-is-the-delivery-lead-time-for-qiaofeng-vertical-machini">What is the delivery lead time for QIAOFENG vertical machining centers?</h3>
  <p>Standard production lead time is <strong>30–45 days</strong> from order confirmation. Worldwide shipping is available via sea freight (most cost-effective for full machines) or air freight for urgent spare parts.</p>
