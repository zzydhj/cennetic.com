<h2 id="pros-and-cons-gantry-cnc-machine-vs-bridge-cnc">Pros and Cons: Gantry CNC Machine vs Bridge CNC</h2>
<h3 id="gantry-cnc-machine">Gantry CNC Machine</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pros:</strong> Handles workpieces up to 50 tons; X-axis travel up to 20 m+; stationary table simplifies fixturing of heavy parts; excellent chip evacuation; scalable for future large-part requirements.</li>
<li><strong>Cons:</strong> Larger floor space required; 20–30% higher initial investment than equivalent bridge machines; slightly lower maximum accuracy (±0.005 mm vs ±0.003 mm).</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="bridge-type-cnc">Bridge Type CNC</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pros:</strong> Tighter tolerances (±0.003 mm); more compact footprint; lower upfront cost; higher maximum spindle speed (up to 30,000 RPM); ideal for medium-sized precision components.</li>
<li><strong>Cons:</strong> Moving table limits workpiece weight (typically ≤20 tons); shorter X-axis travel; less suitable for very large molds or structural aerospace parts.</li>
</ul>
<p>The right choice depends on your dominant part size and weight. If your largest workpieces exceed 5 meters or 20 tons, a <strong>gantry CNC machine</strong> is the clear answer. For high-precision medium parts, a bridge mill delivers superior accuracy at lower cost.</p>

<h2 id="frequently-asked-questions-gantry-cnc-machine-vs-bridge-cnc">Frequently Asked Questions: Gantry CNC Machine vs Bridge CNC</h2>
<h3 id="1-what-is-the-main-difference-between-a-gantry-cnc-machine-a">1. What is the main difference between a gantry CNC machine and a bridge type CNC?</h3>
<p>The primary structural difference is the moving component: in a <strong>gantry CNC machine</strong>, the gantry moves over a stationary table; in a bridge type, the table moves under a stationary bridge. This affects workpiece weight capacity, footprint, and dynamic stiffness. Gantry machines handle very large, heavy parts (up to 50 tons); bridge machines offer slightly higher precision (±0.003 mm) for medium-sized components.</p>
<h3 id="2-which-machine-is-better-for-aerospace-gantry-or-bridge-cnc">2. Which machine is better for aerospace: gantry or bridge CNC?</h3>
<p>For large monolithic aerospace components like wing skins, spars, and fuselage frames, a <strong>gantry CNC machine</strong> is typically superior due to its ability to handle oversized parts in a single setup. For smaller, high-precision aerospace brackets and fittings, a bridge mill may be more cost-effective. Many aerospace suppliers operate both machine types for different part families.</p>
