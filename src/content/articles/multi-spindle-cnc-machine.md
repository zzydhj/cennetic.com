<h2 id="6-frequently-asked-questions">6. Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3 id="what-is-the-main-advantage-of-a-multi-spindle-cnc-machine-ov">What is the main advantage of a multi-spindle CNC machine over a single-spindle?</h3>
<p>The primary advantage is parallel processing. A multi-spindle CNC machine machines several parts simultaneously, drastically reducing cycle time per part. For high-volume production, this typically means 3–5x higher output with the same or fewer operators.</p>

<h3 id="is-a-multi-spindle-cnc-machine-suitable-for-small-batch-prod">Is a multi-spindle CNC machine suitable for small batch production?</h3>
<p>Multi-spindle machines excel in high-volume runs, but modern CNC controls allow relatively quick changeovers. For batches as low as 500 parts—especially repeat orders—the efficiency gains can still outweigh setup time. Contact Cennetic for a batch-size suitability assessment.</p>

<h3 id="how-does-maintenance-compare-between-single-and-multi-spindl">How does maintenance compare between single and multi-spindle machines?</h3>
<p>Multi-spindle machines have more moving parts, making routine maintenance more involved. However, Cennetic’s modular component design speeds up repairs, and our standard <strong>2-year warranty</strong> combined with remote diagnostics minimizes unplanned downtime. Scheduled preventive maintenance intervals are clearly documented in the machine manual.</p>

<h3 id="what-industries-benefit-most-from-multi-spindle-cnc-lathes">What industries benefit most from multi-spindle CNC lathes?</h3>
<p>Automotive, aerospace, medical device, and electronics industries see the greatest gains. Any application requiring high volumes of turned parts—from fasteners and brass fittings to complex shafts and hydraulic components—can benefit significantly.</p>
