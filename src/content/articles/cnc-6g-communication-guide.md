  <ul>
    <li><strong>Real-Time Toolpath Adjustments:</strong> Massive 3D CAD/CAM files and real-time compensation data can be streamed to the machine controller instantly, ensuring zero lag during complex 5-axis aerospace machining.</li>
    <li><strong>AI-Driven Predictive Maintenance:</strong> Our machines are equipped with advanced sensors. When connected via ultra-low latency networks, this sensor data (vibration, temperature) is sent to central AI servers instantly, predicting spindle wear before it affects part quality.</li>
    <li><strong>Seamless Fleet Synchronization:</strong> In large automated production lines, multiple Cennetic CNC centers can communicate with robotic loading arms in real-time, eliminating bottleneck delays.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 id="frequently-asked-questions">Frequently Asked Questions</h3>
  <p><strong>What is CNC 6G communication?</strong><br>
  <strong>CNC 6G communication</strong> refers to the integration of sixth-generation wireless technology (utilizing terahertz frequencies) within smart factory environments. It allows CNC machines, robots, and central servers to exchange massive amounts of data with near-zero latency.</p>

  <p><strong>Do I need 6G to use Cennetic CNC machines?</strong><br>
  No. Cennetic machines are fully functional as standalone units or via standard Ethernet/Wi-Fi. However, their controllers and sensor architectures are designed to be “future-proof,” meaning they can easily integrate into 5G or upcoming 6G networks when your factory upgrades its infrastructure.</p>

  <p><strong>How does low latency improve machining quality?</strong><br>
  In ultra-precision machining, conditions like thermal expansion or tool vibration change in milliseconds. A low-latency network allows the machine’s sensors to send this data to an edge-computing server and receive compensation instructions instantly, ensuring the toolpath remains perfectly accurate.</p>
