    <div>
      <p class="pg-eyebrow">How We Support You</p>
      <h2>Support Comes From the Engineers Who Build the Machines</h2>
      <p>You deal directly with the factory in Dongguan that assembled your machine — not a third-party reseller. That means questions about machining processes, parameters and faults go to people who actually know the machine inside out.</p>
      <p>Most support is handled remotely and resolves quickly. Where a problem genuinely needs hands-on work, we arrange on-site service (availability depends on location, and travel costs may apply).</p>
    </div>
    <div>
      <p class="pg-eyebrow">What's Included</p>
      <ul class="pg-checks">
        <li><strong>Installation &amp; commissioning</strong> — full remote guidance by video call, email and documentation; on-site commissioning can be arranged on request.</li>
        <li><strong>Remote technical diagnostics</strong> — engineers walk you through fault-finding and step-by-step repair over video call or email.</li>
        <li><strong>Free replacement parts</strong> — defective components are shipped to you at no cost within the warranty period.</li>
        <li><strong>Spare-parts supply</strong> — wear parts outside the warranty at competitive prices with fast international shipping.</li>
        <li><strong>Transit damage claims</strong> — cargo-insured shipments with a documented claim process.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="pg-section tint">
    <p class="pg-eyebrow">Warranty</p>
    <h2>What the 2-Year Warranty Covers</h2>
    <div class="pg-cards">
      <div class="pg-card"><h3>Machine body &amp; frame</h3><p>2-year full coverage against manufacturing defects.</p></div>
      <div class="pg-card"><h3>Electrical components &amp; controllers</h3><p>2-year full coverage under normal operating conditions.</p></div>
      <div class="pg-card"><h3>Motors, spindles &amp; mechanical parts</h3><p>2-year full coverage for defects in materials and workmanship.</p></div>
    </div>
    <p class="pg-note"><strong>Not covered:</strong> consumables and normal-wear items (cutting tools, belts, filters), damage from misuse or improper operation, and any machine modified, repaired or disassembled by unauthorized personnel. Full terms are on our <a href="/cnc-machine-refund-policy/">Refund Policy &amp; Warranty page</a>.</p>

  <section class="pg-section pg-2col">
    <div>
      <p class="pg-eyebrow">Getting Help</p>
      <h2>How to Raise a Support or Warranty Request</h2>
      <p>Have your machine model and order number ready. Clear photos or a short video of the issue are required to process any warranty or transit claim quickly.</p>
      <p>Our after-sales team reviews each submission and responds with a resolution plan — replacement parts, a remote repair session, or on-site service where needed.</p>
    </div>
    <div>
      <p class="pg-eyebrow">The Process</p>
      <ul class="pg-checks">
        <li><strong>1. Document the issue</strong> — take photos and/or video showing the defect or damage.</li>
        <li><strong>2. Contact us</strong> — send the details, machine model and order number by WhatsApp or email.</li>
        <li><strong>3. Engineer review</strong> — the after-sales team responds within 3 business days with a resolution plan.</li>
        <li><strong>4. Resolution</strong> — free warranty parts are shipped, or a remote / on-site repair is arranged.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </section>
